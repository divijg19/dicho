# Source material

This file records the provenance of material used to build the curated collection. Original source material stays distinguishable from derived entries in `data/curated/`.

## Primary source

- Work: *Diccionario de refranes, adagios, proverbios, modismos, locuciones y frases proverbiales de la lengua española*
- Author: José María Sbarbi y Osuna, 1834 to 1910
- Edition: Madrid, Librería de los Sucesores de Hernando, 1922, published posthumously
- Status: public domain by author death date
- Verification aids:
  - Internet Archive scan of the 1922 edition, `https://archive.org/details/diccionarioderef01sbaruoft`, accessed 2026-11-26
  - Fundación Carlos Slim edition at `https://pruebat.org/biblioteca/descargar/pdf/Diccionario-de-refranes-adagios-proverbios-modismos-locuciones-y-frases-proverbiales.pdf`, accessed 2026-11-26. Its front matter declares the work public domain, but restricts redistribution of that digital edition outside the foundation, so it is used for verification only.
  - Γνώμη digital corpus at `https://www.gr-gnome.eu/proverbium/`, accessed 2026-11-26, used to cross check individual refranes

Entries refran-021 through refran-040 were checked against the digitized text of the 1922 dictionary. Wording follows the source where the OCR made it recoverable. English translations are our own drafts and are not taken from the source. No author is attributed to the refranes themselves because they are traditional anonymous expressions.

## Earlier entries

Entries refran-001 through refran-020 are traditional anonymous refranes and sayings recorded without a specific collection citation. Their translations are drafts. A collection citation for these entries is still needed.
